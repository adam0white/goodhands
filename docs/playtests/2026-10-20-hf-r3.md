# Good Hands HF playtest, round 3

Nine deterministic HF-character sessions on simulation build `4526847`: `completionist`, `mischief`, and `rulesLawyer`, each on seeds 1–3, capped at 60 modeled real minutes. Raw traces: [2026-10-20-hf-r3.json.gz](2026-10-20-hf-r3.json.gz). Node v26.12.0; installed HF 2.9.0; sibling playtester checkout `9b4c911`, read only.

| Persona       | Result                                                  | Modeled minutes | Mean engagement |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| completionist | All three reached the cap in week four                  | 60              | 0.86            |
| mischief      | All three stopped for their preset agenda in week two   | 19–20           | 0.79            |
| rulesLawyer   | Two agenda stops in week three; seed 2 quit in week two | 21–42           | 0.77            |

No session crashed or finished the month. Completionists stopped Friday 27 November at 18:26 (seed 1), Friday at 08:32 (seed 2), and Sunday 29 November at 08:43 (seed 3). Their histories contain 136, 97, and 121 placement records, including ended placements and replacements. Repeated withdrawal and replacement remain a limitation; this run does not show improved month completion.

The cohort exercised 40 `take-number` inputs and 109 phone interruptions: 48 during interviews and 61 during reference calls. Four sessions encountered the first worker’s Monday evening call. It also exercised 53 negotiations, 42 promises, and 63 withdrawals within conversations. These are exercised actions, not successful arrangements. The adapter exposes only the public View and uses the same inputs as the UI.

There were 48 withdrawn-action warnings, versus 60 in round 2, and no unanswered-input warnings. All nine openings still expose three equally promising interviews. The rubric counts 125–185 words of exposed copy and controls. These are model signals; they do not establish browser visibility, human enjoyment, willingness to return, or owner acceptance.

`npm run check` passed 85 tests before the simulation commit. Regression coverage includes interrupted interviews/references, resumed choices and notes, departure deadlines and contact saving, household names, story uniqueness and evidence tags, the Monday requests and evening call, late evening day-card updates, and both fee installments without duplicate collection. One Arslan placement leaves ₺5,250 before Friday rent and ₺250 afterward.

Five seeds × three public policies all finished the month. Careful retained more workers than reckless in three seeds and earned more warm worker trust in all five. Cunning earned strictly the most cash in all five. Acceptance assertions were retained. Measurement: `scratch/p2-measure-final.jsonl`.
