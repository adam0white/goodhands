**Best moment:** Gül wanting Sundays for her family. That was a person asking for something reasonable, rather than a row of traits. Her crying answer then appeared as “patient · says”, which made my question feel useful. Receiving ₺4,500 for placing her was also a clear result.

**Worst moment:** The warning about Sevda stayed after I interviewed her. It told me to speak without helping me recognize whether I had done the intended thing. I felt responsible for her leaving but could not tell how to respond.

**Boredom / frustration:** At +2:41 I became frustrated by that warning. By +2:53 the one visible request was filled. At +3:01 I was looking through tabs instead of pursuing a clear next action. That was the point I would leave during a break. This was a voluntary stop, with 26:59 left in the budget, not a finished month.

**Goal and next action after the first minute:** I could explain the initial goal: find a nanny before Tuesday and earn Friday's rent.
